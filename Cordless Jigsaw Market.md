# Introduction

The evolution of cordless power tools has transformed the way professionals and hobbyists approach cutting applications. Among the most versatile tools in this category is the **cordless jigsaw**, capable of delivering accurate straight, curved, and intricate cuts across a wide variety of materials.

Unlike conventional corded models, cordless jigsaws provide complete mobility without compromising cutting performance. Improvements in lithium-ion batteries, brushless motors, electronic speed control, and lightweight construction have made these tools more powerful, efficient, and reliable than ever before.

As construction projects expand, furniture manufacturing grows, and DIY home improvement continues to gain popularity, the **Cordless Jigsaw Market** is becoming an increasingly important segment within the global power tools industry.
